# Offer

**Positioning:** Practical AI for small and medium businesses, built by engineers on IT
foundations you can trust. We check that your basics are safe, then build AI that saves time.

## Offer ladder

| Step | Offer | What the client gets | Starting price (test and adjust) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI-Readiness Scan (website) | ~25 questions, instant score per area, top fixes, emailed report | Free (lead magnet) |
| 2 | IT & AI Assessment | Structured review of identity, devices, network, data, apps, monitoring and AI use; findings report + AI opportunity roadmap | €1,500–3,000 fixed |
| 3 | AI Solutions | AI receptionist setup, ask-your-documents assistant, workflow automation | €3,000–15,000 per project |
| 4 | Managed AI | Hosting, monitoring, monthly improvements, support | €99–499 per month |

Prices are starting hypotheses, not market facts. Revisit after the first five sales conversations.

## Why us

- Engineers who build and run it, not just advise.
- Security and IT basics first, so AI does not create new risk.
- Fixed prices and plain language.

## Assessment method (step 2)

Seven areas: foundation, identity, devices, network, applications, data, visibility.
Each item is marked in place / partial / gap / not applicable and prioritised in four phases:
1 immediate risk → 2 core baseline → 3 business systems → 4 advanced (AI governance, cost, automation).
The full item list is maintained privately; the website scan uses a short subset
(`packages/assessment`).
