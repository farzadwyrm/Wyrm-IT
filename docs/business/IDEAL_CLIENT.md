# Ideal client (first version, refine after real conversations)

- **Size:** 5–150 employees. Large enough to feel IT pain, too small for an in-house IT/AI team.
- **Sectors to start with:** clinics and dental practices, law and accounting firms, property
  agencies, trades and home services, small engineering firms.
- **Signals:** missed phone calls, staff re-typing data between tools, "we want AI but don't know
  where to start", no one owns IT, worry about security or GDPR.
- **Buyer:** owner, managing director or office manager.
- **Not a fit (for now):** enterprises with procurement departments; companies wanting AI with no
  budget; regulated work we cannot yet support.

## Questions to ask in every first call

1. Which 3 systems could the business not run without for one day?
2. What repetitive task wastes the most hours each week?
3. How many calls or emails go unanswered?
4. Has anything gone wrong with IT or security in the past year?
5. Is anyone already using AI tools? Which ones, for what?
